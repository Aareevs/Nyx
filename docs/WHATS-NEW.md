# What's new in Nyx

Nyx started from the open-source [Vorssaint](https://github.com/vorssaint/vorssaint-utils) and has since gone its own way. These are the things it has that Vorssaint does not:

The short version is on the [README](../README.md#whats-new-in-nyx). This is the full list.

- **Spotify in the island.** Browse your playlists, reorder them, play any song and see what's up next, all from the island. Needs **Spotify Premium**. See [Spotify in the Dynamic Island](../README.md#spotify-in-the-dynamic-island).
- **YouTube and YouTube Music playlists.** Paste a playlist link and keep it in the island, next to your Spotify playlists. A song plays right inside Nyx with no browser, and the island's player shows it with play, pause, next, previous and seek. Public and unlisted playlists work with no sign-in. YouTube's ads play unless you use the optional **Sign in to YouTube**, so your Premium applies. See [Spotify in the Dynamic Island](../README.md#spotify-in-the-dynamic-island).
- **Time and weather on the island.** The closed island shows the time on the left and the weather, with an emoji, on the right. The weather uses your approximate location if you allow it (Open-Meteo, with MET Norway as a fallback, no account). While a timer runs, the time of day stays on the left beside it, in your 12 or 24 hour format.
- **Calculator in the island.** A new section with three modes: basic arithmetic (powers, roots, fractions), trigonometry (sin, cos, tan, csc, sec, cot and their inverses, in degrees or radians), and calculus (derivatives, integrals, complex numbers, factorials). Shortcut ⌥⌘K.
- **Alarms in the island.** A new section for alarms that ring from the island: set a time with scroll wheels, add a label, and repeat on the days you pick, or ring once. A ringing alarm plays a sound and stays on the closed island with a **Stop** button until you stop it, and the Alarms page can snooze it for 5 minutes. Shortcut ⌥⌘L. Nyx has to be running for an alarm to ring.
- **Add events to Calendar.** The island's Calendar page has a **+** button that opens a small form for a title, date and time, all day, and which calendar to save to. Events go into the Calendar app, so they sync as usual.
- **Teleprompter in the island.** A new section for reading a script while you record or present. Type it in the built-in editor or paste it, set the speed in WPM, and follow the current line with the lines before and after it dimmed, a progress bar and the time left. Lines fill the width of the island, and a resize button on the page switches between the normal and a larger size. It can hide the island from screen recordings and screenshots (on by default; some screen-sharing apps may still show it). Shortcut ⌥⌘E.
- **Streaks in the island.** Add your GitHub, LeetCode and Reddit usernames and the island shows each streak with a flame, whether today is done, and your best run. The closed island greets a streak with its logo and an animated flame, and at a time you pick it rings like an alarm only if a streak is still open, then stops once you have done it. Off by default; it looks the usernames up online (see the privacy notes), turns the day over at your Mac's local midnight (LeetCode counts in UTC like the service), GitHub only counts private contributions if your profile shows them, and reddit.com doesn't allow apps to read a Reddit streak, so for Reddit you tap **Mark done** each day and Nyx counts the days you mark. Shortcut ⌥⌘J.
- **Chat in the island.** Chat with Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or NVIDIA from the Dynamic Island, using your own API key: paste the key once and pick a model. It has a button beside the island, a page of its own (⌥⌘H), and an optional shortcut (Option+Shift+Space by default, off until you switch it on) that attaches whatever text you have selected, where the app shares it. Replies stream in as they are written, code comes in boxes with a copy button, and past chats are kept on your Mac (turn that off, or delete them, in Settings). Keys are kept in a private file on your Mac and messages go straight to the service you chose, with no Nyx server in between. See the privacy notes.
- **Send files to Chat.** Use the paperclip to attach images, PDFs, Word (`.doc`, `.docx`), Excel (`.xlsx`), CSV and plain text or Markdown files, up to 20 MB each. You see a preview before you send and the file stays on the message afterwards. Text is read on your Mac and sent to the service you chose; pictures go as pictures, and a scanned PDF's first pages go as pictures too. Saved chats keep a file's name, not its picture. The old `.xls` format isn't supported.
- **A full color picker.** The Color picker now opens a panel instead of sampling the screen straight away: a gradient square and hue slider, an editable hex value, HEX, RGB and HSL rows with a copy button each, WCAG contrast against white and black, a list of recent colors, and a Pick from screen button.
- **Edit clipboard text in Scratchpad.** Text entries in the island's Clipboard have a pen button that opens the text in Scratchpad.
- **Menu bar folder.** Collapse the menu bar icons you rarely use into one folder, and click through to them without leaving the bar.
- **Antigravity usage.** The AI agents section reads Antigravity's conversations and shows its token use, models and cost, with its own card and the Gemini mark. Also, in settings can turn off showing Antigravity as working.
- **Claude limits fixed up.** Claude plan limits are read more reliably, and limit bars now default to showing how much you have used.
- **Tap the notch to open it (experimental).** With a fingertip, tap the dark notch beside the camera and the island opens, using the ambient light sensor and the motion sensors the way [Sentient OS](https://github.com/Sentient-OS-Labs/sentient-os) does. It is off by default (**Settings → Notch**) and works only on MacBooks that expose these sensors, with the lid open. Touch is done as well as a Mac allows, so expect limits. A MacBook has no touchscreen, so Nyx can't sense where a finger lands; it only notices a fingertip shading the light sensor at the notch plus a small knock of the case:
  - It works best in a normally lit room. The sensor needs some light, so in a dark room, or with a camera cover over it, nothing registers. Click, hover and the shortcut still open the island.
  - Only the notch works. The weather, time and other areas beside it are too far from the sensor, so tapping them does nothing (clicking them with the mouse or trackpad works).
  - Light touches, hovers and half-presses may not register; a firm fingertip does.
  - It is not perfect: a strong breeze, a bump on the desk, someone walking past or the Mac being moved can sometimes open the island by itself, and a tap can occasionally miss. Results differ between Mac models and rooms.
  - It pauses while the Mac sleeps or is locked and starts again after waking, once the lid has settled for a few seconds.
- **MP4 or MOV for compressed videos.** Media tools has a format switch, so a compressed video can be saved as MP4 or MOV.
- **Streaks notice a commit sooner.** A GitHub commit you made today now counts as soon as GitHub lists it, instead of waiting for the profile page to catch up.
- **Spotify fixes.** A playlist whose songs Spotify keeps from apps now says so, and Play still works. When Nyx has to start Spotify for you, it opens it quietly in the Dock with no window in your way. The YouTube player no longer shows up as a second "Nyx" in the source list.
- **Uninstaller: select all.** A checkbox above the results checks or clears every found file at once, and shows a dash when only some are chosen.
- **Uninstaller: cleanup after you drag an app to the Trash.** Optional, in the Uninstaller's settings (needs Full Disk Access). When you move an app to the Trash in Finder, Nyx can offer to clean up its leftover files. It asks before moving anything, and keeps data if the app is only being updated.
- **Uninstaller scanning fix.** App sizes no longer miss folders that sit next to a symbolic link.
- **Notch: full screen and newer macOS.** The island hides over an app's own full screen on every display and eases back afterwards, mirrored notification banners are hidden before they draw, the native banner is hidden on macOS 26 too, and the island stays off status items on macOS 27.
- **Copy image** on Recent Captures and on the Shelf, so a screenshot goes straight to your clipboard.
- **Downloads show their date.** Each finished download in the island lists when it was saved.
- **Check for updates.** **Settings → General → Updates** looks at the Nyx releases page and tells you when a newer version is out, with a Download button. Turn on the daily check if you want it to look on its own. It never installs anything by itself.
- **Smoother, lighter app.** Fan Control, Kill Process, Port Manager, Disk Image Installer, Homebrew management, feedback and link sharing are gone, along with the extra languages (English only), so there is no privileged helper and far less code to trust. The source is about 20% smaller.
